Leave PolicyHRLEC04, Ver 7.0 Interra IT Internal Page 3 of 53.2.2 Privileged Leave• 
Employees will be entitled to 12 days Privileged Leaves (PL) during each calendar year 
which will be credited on monthly basis.• PL will be credited to a new joiner post 
confirmation on monthly basis with the credit of the last six months.(Eg: DOJ – 1st Jan 
2026, Confirmation Date – 30th June 2026, Entitlement of PL from Jan-Jun 2026 will be 6 
andCredit of PL on 1st July 2026 – 6+1.0 = 7.0 PL)• All employes joining on or before 15th 
of the month will be granted full 1 PL and anyone joining post 15thwill be granted 0.5 
PL.• Any employee leaving on or before 15th of the month will be granted 0.5 PL and will 
be granted full 1.0 PL in case the date of exit is post 15th of the month. • All PL must be 
planned and applied in advance seeking First Level Manager’s approval.• PL can be 
availed in multiples of half day as well.• PL can accumulate throughout the calendar

Leave PolicyHRLEC04, Ver 7.0 Interra IT Internal Page 4 of 53.2.5 Maternity Leave• All 
female employees who have worked for 80 days in the company in the preceding 12 
months, are entitled to maternity leave for a period of 26 weeks (182 days) i.e. 8 weeks 
prior to the expected date of delivery and 18 weeks following the delivery day, in 
accordance with the provisions of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 as amended vide 
Gazette Notification No. 934 dated 3 April 2017 only for her first 2 kids. Intervening 
holidays will be included in the calculation of 182 calendar days. If a woman employee
